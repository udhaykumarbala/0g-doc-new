# Eligibility and Whitelist Participation
- **Whitelist Access**: Community engagement, role-based eligibility, and participation in 0G’s Discord or X channels were the pathways to a whitelist spot. Joining the whitelist did not guarantee a purchase; it only ensured allocation access during the whitelist phase.
- **Special Roles and Partnerships**: Selected community members, project partners, and Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs) received additional access or promotional privileges, subject to guidelines for eligible users and without guarantees for resale or transferability of whitelist spots.
